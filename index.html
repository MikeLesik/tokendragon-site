Token DragonTDRN on Ethereum, DWRLD on Solana

Token Dragon will be in-game currency of Dragon Worlds, fantasy MMORPG now in development. It is issued as two separate tokens: TDRN on Ethereum and DWRLD on Solana. Each has fixed supply of 1,000,000, minted once at creation, and neither can mint more.

View on Etherscan View on Solscan

What Token Dragon is for

Dragon Worlds is fantasy MMORPG in development: one shared online world where players build up their characters, gather gear and play together. Token Dragon is planned as its in-game currency.

Players will spend it on character upgrades and in-game items. They will get it as rewards for play, through community programs such as playtests, and from official pools on Uniswap and Orca.

Token Dragon exists as two separate tokens: TDRN on Ethereum and DWRLD on Solana. Each has fixed supply of 1,000,000, minted once at creation. Neither token can mint more, and neither has keys that could freeze holders or take their tokens.

Team

Token Dragon is founded and run by Maksim Vernik. He leads token design, website and litepaper, and product and game design of Dragon Worlds. His background is product management of fintech and Web3 products.

At this stage he is the only team member. Project has no outside investors or advisors, and team will grow as game development progresses.

On Solana team allocation of 150,000 DWRLD was locked on 21 November 2026 in Jupiter Lock vesting contract. Nothing unlocks before 25 November 2027, then 6,250 DWRLD unlocks every month for 24 months. Contract cannot be cancelled and its recipient cannot be changed. Payouts go to project wallet on Solana. On Ethereum team allocation will be locked the same way.
