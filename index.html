Token DragonTDRN on Ethereum

TDRN will be in-game currency of Dragon Worlds, fantasy MMORPG now in development. Fixed supply of 1,000,000 tokens, all minted once at deployment. The contract has no owner and no mint function, so supply can never grow.

View on Etherscan

What TDRN is for

Dragon Worlds is fantasy MMORPG in development. TDRN is planned as its in-game currency: players will spend it on character upgrades and in-game items.
