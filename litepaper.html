Summary

Token Dragon is planned in-game currency of Dragon Worlds, fantasy MMORPG in development. It is issued as two separate tokens, TDRN on Ethereum and DWRLD on Solana, each with fixed supply of 1,000,000.

This paper describes how Token Dragon will be used in the game, how its supply is allocated and what has been done so far.

Dragon Worlds

Dragon Worlds is online role-playing game set in fantasy world. Players create characters, develop them, gather gear and play together with others in one shared world.

Game is in development and has no public build yet.

How Token Dragon is used

Inside the game Token Dragon is currency for things that move player forward:

  • character upgrades;
  • in-game items.

Players will get it in three ways:

  • as rewards for play, paid from player rewards allocation;
  • through community programs such as playtests;
  • from official pools on Uniswap and Orca.

Two networks

Token Dragon exists on Ethereum as ERC-20 token TDRN and on Solana as SPL token DWRLD. These are two independent tokens with separate supply of 1,000,000 each. There is no bridge between them, and both follow the same allocation plan.

Solana token was issued as TDRN on 16 November 2026 and its ticker was changed to DWRLD on 17 November 2026.

Allocation

Same split applies on each network.

On Solana each allocation has had its own address since 21 November 2026, and team tokens are locked in vesting contract. Liquidity tokens will be added to official pool in stages and locked. On Ethereum all allocations still sit in project wallet and will move the same way. Current distribution, addresses and lock links are published on tokendragon.org.

Liquidity

Official pools pair each token with USDC at 1% fee:

  • Ethereum: Uniswap v3 pool, opened on 5 November 2026 with 1,000 TDRN;
  • Solana: Orca Whirlpool, opened on 16 November 2026 with 1,000 DWRLD.

More liquidity will come from liquidity allocation. It will be locked, and lock transactions will be listed on tokendragon.org.

Team

Token Dragon is founded and run by Maksim Vernik. He leads token design, website and litepaper, and product and game design of Dragon Worlds. His background is product management of fintech and Web3 products.

At this stage he is the only team member. Project has no outside investors or advisors, and team will grow as game development progresses.

On Solana team allocation of 150,000 DWRLD was locked on 21 November 2026 in Jupiter Lock vesting contract. Nothing unlocks before 25 November 2027, then 6,250 DWRLD unlocks every month for 24 months. Contract cannot be cancelled and its recipient cannot be changed. Payouts go to project wallet on Solana. On Ethereum team allocation will be locked the same way.
